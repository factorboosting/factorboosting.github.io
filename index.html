Documentation

Methodology without the wall of text

The full documentation remains available as a PDF, but the essentials are now grouped into expandable notes for faster reading.

Dataset scope

The library covers firms listed on NSE and BSE, uses CMIE Prowess as the source, and starts from April 2002 with monthly updates.

Portfolio construction

Factors are formed from India-specific breakpoints and sorted portfolios across size, book-to-market, profitability, investment, and momentum dimensions.

Investible filters

The construction process applies filters and market-structure adjustments so the reported series better reflect tradable stocks and reduce outlier distortion.

Research use

Researchers and practitioners can download returns, inspect stock labels, cite the working paper, and reproduce or extend the factor construction.

About

Rapid advancements in data, compute, and algorithms are profoundly transforming our world, and we’re still in the early stages of this data science, machine learning, and AI revolution.

At SCDLDS, we conduct high-level applied and methodological research in data and learning sciences to aid in informed decision-making and address complex societal challenges. Initially, our work is focused on four key areas: ⁠Data-driven financial research, ⁠Weather and climate modeling, Reinforcement learning, and Epidemiological modeling.
