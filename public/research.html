Working paper October 2026 Portfolio management

Directionally Correct Views in Portfolio Optimization

Ojas Kumar, Sreejit Dey, Sarvesh R. Iyer, and Sandeep Juneja

How valuable is expert information that gets the direction right, even when it is imperfect? This paper shows that modest, directionally correct views can materially improve portfolio allocations. It develops results for Markowitz portfolios, extends the idea to end-to-end machine-learning models, and compares both approaches using Indian equity data.
